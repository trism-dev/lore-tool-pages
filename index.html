Lore Channel Tool

A private, single-user tool that one person uses to run their own YouTube channel of long, slow narrated lore videos for late-night listening.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It plans and narrates each video, uploads it to the owner's channel, and reads that channel's analytics to see which videos work.

It is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and does not access any other YouTube channel or Google account.
